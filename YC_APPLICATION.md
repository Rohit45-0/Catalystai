# Neural Knights: YC application working draft

## One-line description

Neural Knights turns company knowledge into governed internal applications.

## What the company makes

Operations teams explain a problem and connect their policies, records, and internal notes. Neural Knights builds a living map of how the work happens, identifies the highest-impact bottlenecks, recommends purpose-built applications, and deploys the selected app on a safe shared runtime.

The current MVP generates a complaint-risk and escalation desk for fintech operations. It maps complaint records, response policy, team ownership, and manual handoffs; identifies review delays; generates workflow rules; validates them against historical cases; and keeps consequential actions behind human approval.

## What is new

AI app builders begin with a requested interface. Workflow tools begin with a manually configured process. Neural Knights begins with the company's operational evidence and determines what software should exist.

The defensible loop is:

1. Company evidence creates an execution graph.
2. The graph identifies a bottleneck.
3. Neural Knights generates and deploys an application.
4. Human corrections and operational outcomes become evaluations.
5. Those evaluations improve future applications and updates.

## Why now

Models can generate interfaces and workflow logic, but companies still struggle with deployment, permissions, evidence, approvals, maintenance, and deciding what should be built. Neural Knights provides that governed runtime and intelligence layer.

## Current demo

In under 90 seconds a user can load a workspace, inspect the execution map, choose a recommended complaint-operations application, deploy it, approve an evidence-backed escalation, and inspect the audit record.

## Scope after the application

Talk to ten fintech complaint and compliance operators. Replace the synthetic workspace with two permissioned design-partner datasets. Measure complaint-review time, missed internal deadlines, and operator corrections. Build live ingestion only for the systems those partners already use.
